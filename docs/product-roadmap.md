# Product roadmap

| Priority | Work | Acceptance evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Label a stratified sample of recent English and Turkish publisher reports, with a smaller German, French and Spanish challenge set. | Two reviewers agree on a taxonomy guide and publish agreement, precision, recall and confusion by topic. |
| 1 | Calibrate impact against editorial judgments and improve extraction for quantitative and verified institutional consequences. | Review the score distribution and compare ranking relevance on the same labeled sample. Track abstention and provisional cases by category and language. |
| 2 | Review real event pairs, including recurring sports fixtures, wire copies, bilingual coverage and adjacent developments. | Evaluate pairwise precision and recall across multiple publication windows. Preserve publisher and underlying wire attribution where feeds supply it. |
| 2 | Decide whether durable storage is justified by expired article links, cold starts and audit retention. | Agree on operating cost and data retention before selecting infrastructure. Retain an RSS-only mode. |
| 3 | Add optional topic following if saved-view usage and direct feedback justify it. | Global agenda remains independently ranked and local preferences can be reset. |
| 3 | Add production quota and outcome monitoring with appropriate access. | Verify authenticated cron delivery, provider degradation, concurrency behavior and release health during an approved rollout. |
